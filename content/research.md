My research is driven by the goal of making language technologies and knowledge **more accessible across languages, communities, and resource constraints**. I explore how models can transfer and generalize capabilities across languages and tasks through **cross-lingual alignment, representation learning, and efficient adaptation**. I also work on improving how systems represent, retrieve, and utilize relevant information, from **neural retrieval to evidence-grounded language generation**. Across these directions, I aim to develop methods that reduce dependence on language-specific resources and task-specific solutions, enabling more reliable and adaptable technologies for diverse real-world needs.
